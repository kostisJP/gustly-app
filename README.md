# Gustly

Gustly is a GPS session tracker for **windsurfing, kitesurfing, wingfoiling, and windfoiling**. Record your time on the water, see where your fastest runs happened, and track your progress across sessions.

Visit [gustly.app][website] to learn more about the app.

This repository is Gustly’s issue tracker. Use it to report bugs, suggest improvements, and follow updates on issues you care about.

**[Report a bug][bug] · [Suggest a feature][feature] · [Browse issues][issues]**

## What Gustly does

- **Record or import sessions.** Record on your phone, Apple Watch, or a compatible Garmin watch. Import FIT and GPX files from other GPS devices.
- **Review speed and technique.** Explore your route and speed timeline. See your best 2-second speed, 5 × 10-second average, Alpha 500, and nautical-mile speed. Analyze your gybes and tacks.
- **Track and share your progress.** Compare sessions, track personal bests and gear, share a route and stats card, or upload selected sessions to GPS-Speedsurfing (GP3S).

[App Store][ios] · [Google Play][android] · [Garmin Connect IQ][garmin]

## Filing an issue

Search [existing issues][issues] first. If you find a match, add a 👍 reaction or comment with new details. If you open an issue, use a short, specific title and cover one bug or idea per issue.

- **Bug reports.** Include the steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected, what happened, and your device model, OS version, and Gustly version. For recording, sync, or import problems, say how the session got into Gustly. For a speed or distance discrepancy, include the metric, values, and units you’re comparing.
- **Feature requests.** Describe what you want to do, when you’d use it, and how you handle it today.

Other GitHub users with access to this repository can see your posts. Remove personal details from screenshots and keep account information and GPS files out of issues. For account help or to share a session file privately, email [support@gustly.app][support].
